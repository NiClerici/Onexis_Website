# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Swiss and international IT decision-makers — CIOs, IT leaders, programme sponsors,
PMO heads — in insurance, public administration, energy, IT service providers and
broader services. They typically arrive when a critical IT programme is wobbling
(behind plan, unclear reporting, vendor conflict) or when they need senior capacity
on a mandate (interim, PMO, architecture target picture, health check). They are
evaluating whether ONEXIS is a credible, senior partner they can trust with a
high-stakes vorhaben — the visit is a trust assessment, not a transaction.

## Product Purpose

ONEXIS GmbH (Gelterkinden, CH) leads and steers IT vorhaben from planning to
operational handover — nationally and internationally. Services: project leadership,
PMO build/run, interim management, health checks, IT/enterprise architecture, and
seminars. The site exists to communicate senior credibility and a clear method
(the four-dimension Target Operating Model) and to convert a qualified visitor into
a first conversation ("Erstgespräch innerhalb von 48 Stunden, ohne Vertriebsschleife").
Success = the right decision-makers feel "these are the people who bring a
troubled programme home" and reach out.

## Brand Personality

Pragmatic, senior, calm-Swiss-precise, honest. Voice is confident without bluster —
"wir sind pragmatisch", "ohne Vertriebsschleife", "wir sagen ehrlich, ob wir die
Richtigen sind". Three words: **pragmatic, senior, composed.** The interface should
evoke trust, competence under pressure, and clarity — the feeling of a steady hand
on a difficult programme. No hype, no hard sell.

## Anti-references

- **Loud agency / maximalist:** no acid colors, no motion-everywhere, no experimental
  layouts. Swiss calm wins.
- **Big-4 / corporate-brochure consultancy:** avoid faceless navy-and-grey stock
  imagery and generic corporate boilerplate. Keep it sharper and more human.
- **Cheap template look:** nothing that reads as an off-the-shelf Wix/Squarespace
  theme.
- (Implied, from the shared bans) no gradient text, hero-metric SaaS templates,
  identical icon-card grids, or decorative glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

- **Show senior judgement, don't claim it.** Anonymized but concrete case outcomes
  (90 days to stabilization, 8 weeks to decision) earn trust better than adjectives.
- **Method as reassurance.** The TOM model and the five-step Vorgehen exist to make a
  chaotic situation feel governable — structure is the product.
- **Composure over spectacle.** One well-orchestrated hero load and a single
  signature interaction (the TOM circle) beat motion on every section.
- **Honesty as differentiator.** Copy and UX should feel low-pressure and
  straight-talking — the opposite of a sales funnel.
- **Identity-preservation.** The ONEXIS brandsheet (Anthrazit #3C3C3C + teal #62BDCC,
  light-weight humanist-sans headings) is committed identity, not a greenfield choice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast (≥3:1 large text), full keyboard
operability with visible focus, labelled form controls, and a working
`prefers-reduced-motion` path for the hero and TOM animations. German-language site
(`lang="de"`).
